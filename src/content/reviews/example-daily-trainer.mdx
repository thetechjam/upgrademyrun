The alarm goes off at 5:12, the street is still blue, and the last thing you want is a shoe that asks questions. The Example Daily Trainer answers them before you lace up: plenty of foam, a calm ride, and a fit that disappears after the first quarter mile.

## Fit & Feel

Step-in comfort is immediate. The engineered mesh upper wraps the midfoot snugly while leaving generous room for toes to splay, which matters on long runs when feet swell. The heel counter is padded but not bulky, and the collar holds the ankle without rubbing. Our one niggle is the gusseted tongue: it is plush, but on runs past ninety minutes it crept toward the outside of the foot. A firm runner's knot solved it.

True to size for most of our testers. Runners with narrow feet may want to try a half size down or lean on the extra eyelet.

## On the Run

This is a shoe built around consistency. The supercritical EVA blend has a soft initial sink followed by a noticeable rebound, so easy miles feel cushioned without going flat. At marathon effort the 8 mm drop and gentle rocker keep transitions smooth, and the wide platform gives tired legs a stable base.

Push the pace toward threshold and the weight starts to show. At 9.6 ounces it is not sluggish, but it would rather cruise than sprint. Strides and short pickups are fine; full interval sessions are better saved for something lighter.

On wet roads the full-coverage rubber was confidence-inspiring. Painted crosswalks and slick leaves at dawn never caught us out.

Long runs are where it really earns its keep. On a twenty-mile Sunday effort the foam kept its bounce deep into the final hour, and the forgiving landing took some of the sting out of tired, sloppy strides. It is also quiet underfoot, which sounds trivial until you are the only runner on the street at 5:30 a.m.

## Durability

After roughly 320 miles across three testers, the outsole shows only light wear at the lateral heel, and the midsole still feels close to new. The mesh has held up without pilling or tearing at the flex point. We expect most runners to see 400 to 500 miles before the foam starts to feel tired, which is strong value at this price.

## Who It's For

If you run most days and want a single shoe to handle the bulk of your training, the Example Daily Trainer is an easy pick. It suits beginners building mileage and experienced runners who want a reliable partner for easy days and long runs. Speed-focused racers and runners who prefer a firm, ground-feel ride should pair it with a lighter tempo shoe or look elsewhere.
